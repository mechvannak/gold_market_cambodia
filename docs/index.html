Gold Forecast Bot

A Telegram bot and Mini App that reads world economic news, the US dollar, bond yields and price action, then suggests where to buy and sell gold.

View on GitHub Setup guide

How the forecast works

40%NewsRecent headlines on the Fed, inflation, wars, oil and central banks, scored for gold impact by Claude.
30%MacroDollar index, US 10-year yield and VIX. A weaker dollar and falling yields favour gold.
30%TechnicalTrend, 20-day momentum and RSI of gold futures.

What you get

BUY ZONE
Pullback area
SELL ZONE
Target area

Plus a stop-loss, reward/risk ratio, expected price for tomorrow, 1 week and 1 month, and a daily morning alert in Telegram.

Run your own

  1. Create a bot with @BotFather and get a Claude API key.
  2. Clone the repo, copy .env.example to .env and fill in the keys.
  3. Expose the server over HTTPS and set WEBAPP_URL.
  4. Run python main.py and send /start to your bot.

Educational model output, not financial advice. Gold can move sharply on unexpected news. Never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
